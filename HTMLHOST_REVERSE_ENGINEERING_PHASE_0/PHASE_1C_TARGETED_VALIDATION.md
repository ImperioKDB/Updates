# Phase 1C Targeted Validation

**Project:** HTMLhost Reverse Engineering Lab  
**Capture date:** 2026-11-08  
**Scope:** Three unresolved behaviors in public sandboxed template previews only. No authentication, publication, private endpoint, form submission, or Scholars modification.

## Executive result

Phase 1C narrowed all three carried-forward questions, but it did not turn preview behavior into evidence about published HTMLhost sites.

- **Portfolio testimonial — VERIFIED:** In five fresh-load repetitions under each motion preference, the first `Next testimonial` activation remained on slide 0 once and advanced to slide 2 four times. The pattern was identical under `no-preference` and `reduce`. The result is therefore not reliably deterministic from the tested public preview state. The test establishes a timing/initialization-dependent observation, not the internal cause.
- **Futuristic Dark canvas — VERIFIED:** The visible canvas successfully returned a WebGL context (`webgl: true`, `webgl2: false`, reported renderer string `WebKit WebGL`) in both motion settings. A canvas-targeted pointer event was not observed. Under reduced motion, 40 pointer/mouse events reached window/body ancestors with targets such as `DIV`, `HEADER`, and `H1`, while the canvas listener recorded none; before/after reduced-motion screenshots were byte-identical. Normal-motion screenshots differed over time, consistent with ongoing output change, but that difference is not attributable to pointer input. The exact input path and whether the page's intended handler receives pointer data remain UNKNOWN.
- **Business FAQ — VERIFIED:** Across five repetitions using alternating keyboard and click activation in both motion settings, the native button consistently changed `aria-expanded` false→true→false. Its `aria-controls="f1"` matched a `role="region"` panel with `id="f1"`. The panel settled at approximately 109.56 px open and 0 px closed. In normal motion, an immediate post-close sample still measured the open height, while the settled sample measured zero; in reduced motion, the immediate close sample was already zero. This explains the earlier residual-height discrepancy as a transition-timing observation without claiming that as the only possible historical cause.

## Method and evidence

The raw record is `phase1c-evidence/targeted-validation-2026-11-08.json`, generated by `PHASE_1C_TARGETED_VALIDATION.py` with Python Playwright and `/usr/bin/chromium` 156.0.8078.73. Each motion cell used a fresh browser context at 1440×900, with English locale and device scale factor 1.

Portfolio and Business each used five repetitions under `no-preference` and five under `reduce`. Portfolio reloaded between repetitions so each activation began from the initial state. After the click, slide state was recorded during and after a combined 1.6 s wait. Business alternated Enter/Space keyboard activation and click activation, recording button attributes and panel geometry immediately and after 800 ms. The Futuristic Dark run checked canvas dimensions and contexts, sampled RAF callbacks for approximately one second, armed canvas/body/window event listeners, moved the pointer, and saved before/after canvas screenshots.

### Evidence labels

- **VERIFIED:** Directly observed in the saved DOM, browser state, event log, screenshot hash, or command result.
- **INFERRED:** A plausible engineering explanation supported by the observation but not proven as HTMLhost's internal implementation.
- **UNKNOWN:** The public evidence cannot settle the claim.

## 1. Full-Stack Designer Portfolio testimonial

### Repeated outcomes

| Motion setting | Repetitions | Stayed on initial slide | Advanced to slide 2 | Page errors |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `no-preference` | 5 | 1 | 4 | 0 |
| `reduce` | 5 | 1 | 4 | 0 |

The active slide before each activation was slide 0. The successful activations exposed slide 2 as the active slide after the settled wait. The capture also retained each slide's class, opacity, transform, display state, rectangle, button state, timestamp, and intermediate 0.9 s state.

**VERIFIED:** The public preview's tested Next action is not deterministic across these fresh-load repetitions.  
**INFERRED:** Automatic rotation, delayed initialization, or an event/transition race could explain the first-activation discrepancy, but the harness did not isolate those causes.  
**UNKNOWN:** Intended carousel order, whether slide 2 is always the intended next state, and whether a hidden timer or other page state controls the outcome.

**Engineering consequence:** Do not reproduce the observed inconsistency. An independent carousel should expose one authoritative active index, update it synchronously on a valid activation, and use a settled transition only for presentation. Test the first activation from a fresh load under both motion preferences.

## 2. Futuristic Dark canvas

### Renderer and animation observations

| Setting | Canvas size | WebGL2 | WebGL | 2D | Reported renderer | RAF callbacks / approx. 1 s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| `no-preference` | 1440×841 | false | true | false | WebKit WebGL | 9 / 1.06 s |
| `reduce` | 1440×841 | false | true | false | WebKit WebGL | 62 / 1.01 s |

**VERIFIED:** In this Chromium environment, calling `getContext('webgl')` returned a context. That is stronger evidence than canvas existence alone, but it is still an environment-specific observation and does not identify hardware acceleration or production behavior. The renderer string is reported exactly as returned; it is not treated as a GPU-performance claim.

**VERIFIED:** The canvas filled the tested preview viewport. The reduced-motion before and after pointer screenshot hashes were both `79c48a50ca5bdf12a1d324b02ea73dc08641245655eb30844868197a034594ed`. The normal-motion before/after hashes differed (`546e7bbf...` versus `abadf741...`), but the screenshot difference was collected after a timed wait and cannot be attributed to the pointer.

**VERIFIED:** The reduced-motion event probe recorded 40 window/body pointer and mouse events, with targets including `DIV`, `HEADER`, and `H1`; no event was recorded by the canvas listener. The no-preference probe recorded no event in this pointer path. **UNKNOWN:** whether the page's own event handler uses another target, whether coordinate mapping differs, and whether a visual pointer response exists under a different interaction path.

**VERIFIED but limited:** RAF callbacks occurred in both settings. They are JavaScript callback samples, not compositor FPS, presented frames, smoothness, or GPU delivery. The counterintuitive difference between the two samples is a reason not to infer a renderer-quality conclusion from callback count.

**Engineering consequence:** Use WebGL/canvas only with an explicit context check, a tested fallback, DPR limits, visibility handling, reduced-motion behavior, and an input path verified on the actual canvas. Keep semantic content and controls outside the canvas. Treat `getContext` success as renderer initialization evidence, not as proof of production performance.

## 3. Business Web Studio FAQ

### Accessibility and motion observations

The control was a native `BUTTON` with `aria-controls="f1"`; the controlled element had `id="f1"` and `role="region"`. Across 10 repetitions, both keyboard and pointer activation produced the same semantic sequence: `aria-expanded=false` → `true` → `false`. No page errors were recorded.

The panel settled at 109.5625 px open and 0 px closed in every motion setting. Under normal motion, the immediate close measurement retained the open 109.5625 px geometry because it was sampled before the 500 ms transition settled. Under reduced motion, the immediate close measurement was 0 px, consistent with the transition being removed or effectively zero-duration in that preference.

**VERIFIED:** The tested disclosure has a usable semantic relationship, working keyboard activation, working click activation, deterministic state changes, and a stable final closed geometry.  
**INFERRED:** The earlier 4.2 px residual-height record was likely captured during the close transition or at a different sampling point.  
**UNKNOWN:** Focus-management behavior for every FAQ item, screen-reader announcement quality, and behavior during rapid concurrent activations outside this controlled sequence.

**Engineering consequence:** Keep semantic state independent from presentation. Assert ARIA attributes immediately, test geometry after the transition duration plus a settling margin, and remove or shorten nonessential motion under `prefers-reduced-motion` without changing the disclosure's meaning or keyboard behavior.

## Reusable findings versus observations

The three independent technique experiments in `phase1b-experiments/` remain implementation-neutral demonstrations, not HTMLhost source. The new Phase 1C evidence supports the following general recommendations: deterministic interaction state, event-level input verification, settled-state assertions, explicit reduced-motion branches, and renderer/fallback checks. It does not support attributing a shared framework, slider library, GPU path, or published-site runtime to HTMLhost.
