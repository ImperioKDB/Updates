# MTN Foundation Science & Technology Scholarship — evidence note

Audit date: 2026-11-07. This is a read-only editorial review; no production records or systems were changed.

## Findings

The MTN Foundation Science and Technology Scholarship exists. MTN Nigeria’s official scholarships page describes the MTN Foundation Scholarships Program as an annual program, names the Science and Technology Scholarship (STS), says STS has been offered since 2010, describes eligible Nigerian public tertiary institution students, and identifies a 2026 edition awarding 400 scholarships of ₦300,000 until graduation subject to grade and upskilling requirements. Its eligibility section describes relevant levels and CGPA thresholds. The page links directly to an MTN-owned applications subdomain. The portal states “Submission is closed.” Therefore the program’s existence and current closed portal status are supported, but not the specific dates for the 2026 cycle or any 2027 cycle.

Instructions classification: PARTIAL. Supported eligibility and the official portal route can be summarized, but the sources do not state a complete application procedure, required-document list, or an upcoming application window. The proposed instructions below avoid filling these gaps by inference.

Destination assessment: The stored `https://www.mtn.ng/scholarships/` is the provider’s current scholarship page and links to `https://apps.mtn.ng/scholarships/form`; retain it. The official page was reachable in the browser. A text extraction request alone failed to resolve the MTN hostname, which is not evidence of a broken page. The linked portal itself was opened and explicitly reports closed submissions. No URL replacement is proposed.

Deadline/lifecycle: The database snapshot’s 2027-05-31 deadline is not confirmed by the official sources reviewed. The page still describes the 2026 edition, and its linked portal says submissions are closed. No official 2027 opening or closing date was found. The snapshot’s 2026-05-31 last-cycle close date is also not substantiated by the two official sources inspected. Recommend clearing or withholding the future date until MTN publishes an official date; do not present the application as open.

Verification date: SET_DATE to 2026-11-07, because today’s review checked MTN’s official program page and application portal and directly confirmed the program and closed status.

## Supported claims

- The provider identifies the scholarship as part of an annual MTN Foundation Scholarships Program and says STS has been offered since 2010.
- MTN’s page describes STS eligibility among Nigerian public tertiary institution students, including relevant STEM and medical study levels. It lists different CGPA/qualification thresholds by study group.
- MTN’s page describes the 2026 edition as 400 awards worth ₦300,000 until graduation, subject to grade and upskilling requirements.
- The official application form page currently says submissions are closed.
- MTN’s official scholarship page links to the official application form at `apps.mtn.ng`.

## Unresolved claims

- No reviewed official source establishes the precise 2026 application opening or closing date.
- No reviewed official source establishes a 2027 cycle, its dates, or its current status.
- The sources do not provide a complete application workflow or identify documents required for applying.

## Editorial proposals (not production changes)

- Instructions: “The MTN Foundation Science and Technology Scholarship is for eligible Nigerian students in public tertiary institutions. MTN’s listed STS eligibility includes students progressing from 200 level to 300 level or currently in 300 level in science and technology-related courses, and medical students progressing from 300 level to 400 level or currently in 400 level. MTN lists a minimum CGPA of 3.0/4 or 3.5/5 for STEM students in public universities and colleges of education; polytechnic applicants need at least 3.0 from OND and admission to HND; direct-entry STEM applicants need at least 3.0 from OND and admission to 300 level in a public university; medical students need a Pass or equivalent. Apply only through the official MTN portal when a cycle is open. The portal currently says submissions are closed. MTN has not published the next cycle’s dates on the reviewed pages.”
- URL: retain the provider-linked `https://www.mtn.ng/scholarships/`; no URL change proposed.
- Deadline: do not retain 2027-05-31 as established fact without an official announcement; leave a future date unset pending authoritative publication.
