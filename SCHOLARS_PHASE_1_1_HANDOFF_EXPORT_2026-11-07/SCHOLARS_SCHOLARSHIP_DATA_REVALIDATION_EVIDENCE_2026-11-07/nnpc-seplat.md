# NNPC/Seplat JV National Undergraduate Scholarship — evidence note

**Audit date:** 2026-11-07

## Findings

The NNPC/SEPLAT JV National Undergraduate Scholarship exists. Seplat Energy published an official 2026 cycle announcement dated 23 February 2026. It described eligibility, eligible courses, a free online application, and linked applicants to the recorded application URL. Seplat’s accompanying official PDF gives the 2026 application window as **23 February–13 March 2026**. That cycle had ended before this audit date. The official sources reviewed did not establish a 2027 cycle or its opening/deadline dates. The database’s 2027-02-23 opening and 2027-03-13 deadline are unsupported by the reviewed official evidence and resemble the 2026 dates shifted forward one year.

The recorded URL, `https://seplatgrowthacademy.com/scholarship/`, is explicitly linked from Seplat Energy’s official 2026 announcement and PDF, so it is provider-linked and correct for that documented cycle. The page was reachable during review, but its extracted content showed only site navigation, not a current application form or cycle notice. Reachability does not establish that applications are open or that the page is the active route for any future cycle.

**Instructions classification:** PARTIAL. There is authoritative evidence for complete 2026-cycle eligibility and application steps, but no confirmed current/2027-cycle instruction set. Historical instructions should be clearly dated rather than presented as currently actionable.

## Proposals — not production changes

- Keep the stored URL; no authoritative evidence supports changing it.
- Proposed instructions, explicitly labeled as historical: “For the 2026 cycle (23 February–13 March 2026; closed), applicants had to be in at least their second year at a Federal or State university in Nigeria, have a CGPA of at least 3.5, and have five O’level credit passes including English and Mathematics in one sitting. Eligible courses were Accountancy, Agriculture, Architecture, Business Administration, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Computer Engineering/Science, Economics, Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Environmental Studies, Geology, Geophysics, Law, Mass Communication, Mechanical Engineering, Medicine, Metallurgical Engineering, Ophthalmology/Optometry, and Petroleum Engineering. Applications were free and submitted online at https://seplatgrowthacademy.com/scholarship/. Applicants needed a valid personal email; only shortlisted applicants would be notified; terms and conditions applied. No 2027 cycle or current application dates have been confirmed by the sources reviewed.”
- Do not carry the 2026 cycle dates into 2027 without an official announcement. The source review supports setting the verification date to 2026-11-07, but it does not support new 2027 dates.
