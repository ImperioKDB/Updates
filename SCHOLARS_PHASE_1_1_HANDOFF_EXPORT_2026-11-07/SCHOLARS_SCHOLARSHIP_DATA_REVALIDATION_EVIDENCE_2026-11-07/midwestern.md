# Midwestern Oil & Gas Host Community Scholarship — evidence note

**Audit/access date:** 2026-11-07

## Findings

The scholarship program exists. Midwestern Oil & Gas Company Limited’s official announcement invites students to its 2026/2027 scholarship schemes with joint venture partner SunTrust Atlantic Energies and links to its recruitment landing page. The linked 2026/2027 University Community Award application page identifies host communities as the eligible group, describes requirements and submission steps, calls Scholastica its official platform, and explicitly says the application period has ended. It sets the deadline at April 19, 2026. Thus the opportunity is not currently open as of the audit date. No authoritative source reviewed publishes a subsequent cycle’s opening or deadline.

The stored URL `https://candidate.scholastica.ng` is the correct platform domain and is expressly identified on the scheme page as the official platform, but it is only the platform home page, not the direct application destination. The official Midwestern announcement routes applicants through `https://recruitment.dragnet-solutions.com/midwestern/`; that linked landing page routes the University Community Award specifically to `https://candidate.scholastica.ng/schemes/2026midwesterncommunity`. That exact provider-linked route now displays a closed-cycle notice. The 2026/2027 application requirements and steps are documented, but must not be presented as actionable for a new cycle without a new official announcement. Therefore the instructions classification is PARTIAL and the proposed instruction field is left empty pending a live cycle announcement.
