# NNPC–Renaissance JV University Scholarship — revalidation note

**Audit date:** 2026-11-07. **Scope:** Public scholarship information only; read-only review. No production data changed.

## Findings

The program exists. Renaissance Africa Energy’s official Education & Scholarships page describes the NNPC Limited–Renaissance JV University Scholarship as an annual undergraduate grant and identifies National Merit and Areas of Operation Merit award categories. It lists eligibility for the 2026 scheme. The official Scholastica scheme page identifies the same 2026 scholarship and says the application period has ended and candidates can no longer apply. Thus, the scheme’s existence is confirmed, but no currently open or forthcoming cycle is evidenced by these pages. The database’s 2027-05-31 deadline is not supported by the reviewed primary sources.

The database URL, `https://candidate.scholastica.ng`, is a reachable, legitimate application-platform homepage, and Scholastica is linked as the application platform by its scheme record. However, the root page is generic and, when reviewed, lists a different scholarship rather than this one. The scheme page itself publishes the program-specific route `https://candidate.scholastica.ng/schemes/NNPCRENAISSANCEJV2026`, which resolves but explicitly reports that the 2026 application period ended. Do not present that expired route as an open application. No current-cycle application URL is established, so no replacement application URL is proposed.
