## Findings

The scheme exists. PTDF's official Scholarship Management Portal publishes 2026/27 In-Country Scholarship Scheme guidelines explicitly covering undergraduate awards at Nigerian public universities. Those guidelines publish an application window of 27 April to 5 June 2026. That window had elapsed by the audit date; no authoritative 2027/28 opening or deadline was found. The record's stored future dates (20 April 2027 opening and 29 May 2027 deadline) are not substantiated by the reviewed primary sources.

The stored destination `https://scholarship.ptdf.gov.ng` is the official, provider-linked portal: PTDF's own guidelines point applicants there, and the portal itself provides the current guidelines and account/login links. The portal homepage and guideline page were readable through public page fetching. That reachability establishes neither an open application nor the current functioning of all application systems. The homepage displays an undated NIMC server maintenance notice.

**Instructions classification: PARTIAL.** The official 2026/27 requirements and account/NIN procedure are documented, but that cycle is over and no following-cycle instructions are verified. Retrospective, cycle-qualified instructions are proposed below; they must not be presented as an open current intake.

## Proposals — editorial only; not production changes

**Instructions proposal:** The PTDF 2026/27 In-Country Scholarship application window was 27 April–5 June 2026 and has passed. No subsequent cycle dates have been confirmed in the reviewed official sources. For the 2026/27 undergraduate award, applicants had to be full-time students at a public university, currently in 200 level, studying an advertised eligible course, with at least five WAEC/SSCE/NECO credits including English and Mathematics (up to two sittings), a CGPA of at least 2.4/5.0, and first-year examinations completed. The listed uploads were qualifying examination results, university admission letter, signed and stamped first-year transcript, certificate of indigeneship showing state and local government, and a personal statement of at least 300 words. The official process was to create an applicant account on the PTDF Scholarship Management Portal, follow the emailed login/password instructions, log in, verify NIN, and proceed through the application disclaimer. PTDF says NIN verification charges may apply; its portal currently displays an undated NIMC maintenance notice. Check newly published PTDF guidelines before relying on any of these dates or requirements for a future cycle.

**URL proposal:** None. Retain the supplied URL; it is confirmed as the official provider-linked portal.
**Lifecycle proposal:** Do not represent 20 April 2027 / 29 May 2027 as announced dates on this evidence. Record the 2026/27 published window as historical if the data model supports it. Actual portal closure was not separately confirmed; do not claim an observed closure event beyond the published end date having passed.
**Verification-date proposal:** SET_DATE to 2026-11-07 because today's official-source review confirmed the existence of the scheme, the 2026/27 published window, and that the window had passed; it did not verify a new cycle.
