## Findings

The JG Afrika 2027 bursary program exists. Its official bursary page says applications for the 2027 academic year would open in August 2026, names eligible qualifications as BSc/BEng or MSc/MEng in Civil Engineering, and currently presents a detailed 2027 application form. JG Afrika’s official LinkedIn post says the 2027 programme is open and directs applicants to its website. The public link from that post resolves to the same specific bursary URL stored in the record. Together, the live provider form and announcement support treating the current cycle as open as of the audit date; a page loading successfully alone would not establish that status.

The stored URL is correct and current: it is the provider’s bursary page and contains the 2027 application form. The page fetch succeeded. No replacement URL is proposed. The form contains applicant, requested-bursary, education, achievements and guardian sections; it requires a complete academic transcript from first year through current study, a motivation paragraph, a copy of ID and a Matric certificate, and asks applicants to disclose other funding. It warns that incomplete applications or missing documents will not be processed. It states that applicants without confirmation by 01 January 2027 should assume they were unsuccessful; that is not stated as an application deadline.

**Instructions classification:** ACTIONABLE. **Deadline:** not published on the official sources reviewed. **Opening:** August 2026 published; official post confirms applications are now open. **Closing:** no closing date found. **Verification date recommendation:** SET_DATE to 2026-11-07, because the provider page and current-cycle announcement were reviewed on that date.
