# Florence Bamidele Makanjuola Scholarship — revalidation note

**Audit/access date:** 2026-11-07

## Findings

The Florence Bamidele Makanjuola Scholarship exists as an Opekete Foundation program. The Foundation’s official application page describes the FBM Scholarship for Women and publishes eligibility and academic criteria. Its official homepage also describes the fourth scholarship cycle and links to the application page/form. The page’s prominently displayed 2026 application period was May 1–22, 2026, which had ended by the audit date. The official linked Google Form is titled “2026/27 Opekete Foundation Scholarship Application Form” and says it is no longer accepting responses. Thus, the linked route is provider-authorized but is not currently accepting applications. No next-cycle opening/deadline was found in the reviewed provider pages; the 2027-05-01 opening and 2027-05-22 deadline in the supplied database snapshot are not supported by those sources.
