## Findings

The programme exists. The provider’s official scholarships page describes three tuition-support categories: medicine through the G&G award, nursing through the JSI award, and allied-health programmes through the Vision award. Each category is described for students at government institutions. The same page states that the 2026 application window ran from 6 July 2026 through 11:59 PM WAT on 2 October 2026, and that late or incomplete applications would not be considered. That window had passed on the audit date.

The official page also says recipients would receive $100 paid to the university or institutional account, subject to the stated exceptions, and that applicants would be notified by email by 18 December 2026. The page contains a provider-hosted Google Form link in its public HTML, but the extracted page does not establish that the form is still accepting responses. Do not represent the 2026 form or deadline as a currently open application.

The stored URL `https://lightandbellshealth.com/scholarships-and-grants` is the provider’s official scholarship page and remains the correct information destination. No replacement URL is proposed. The stored deadline of 2026-10-02 matches the provider’s published deadline. The missing `last_cycle_closed_at` should not be filled with an invented timestamp; the published deadline establishes that the window ended, but no separate closure event was found.
