# ASUU Scholarship Scheme for Indigent Students — revalidation evidence

**Audit/access date:** 2026-11-07
**Record slug:** `asuu-scholarship-scheme-for-indigent-students-31cfebd0`
**Scope:** Public scholarship information only. No production data was changed; no student data is reproduced here.

## Findings

The scholarship is real. ASUU National's official homepage identifies an indigent scholarship and says 158 students benefited. Independent official university sources also report ASUU branch awards, including an official 2025 KWASU report that distinguishes national ASUU awardees from KWASU-branch awardees. The ASUU National homepage links a “Scholarship Award” post dated 27 August 2025, but the linked page currently contains only its title and comment controls, not eligibility, application steps, or an application destination.

A current-cycle example is published on the official Federal University of Kashere (FUK) account: its ASUU branch invited FUK students to apply for the 2026 ASUU Indigent Scholarship Award, described as sponsored by ASUU's National Body. It specified FUK registration, genuine financial need, minimum CGPA 3.0, and five documents; the call instructed delivery to the FUK ASUU Branch Secretariat or electronic submission to addresses “stated on the notice.” It set a deadline of 24 September 2026 at 12:00 noon. That deadline had passed by the audit date. These terms are explicitly FUK-specific and do not establish the requirements, closing date, or status for every university branch.

The record's 2026-09-30 deadline is already past as of the audit date, but no authoritative source reviewed confirms it as a common or national deadline. It differs from the date in FUK's official branch call. Do not replace it with FUK's date as a national-wide deadline. The existing document list, branch committee/interview, two nominees, and universal application route are not corroborated for the general program by the reviewed primary sources.
