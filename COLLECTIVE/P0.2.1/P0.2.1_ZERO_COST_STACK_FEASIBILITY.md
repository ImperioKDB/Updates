## 1. Executive verdict

**Overall classification: NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 as a verified complete production-capable MVP stack.**

The investigation found useful free or conditionally free building blocks, but no candidate combination satisfies all of Collective's required conditions at once: authenticated objective submission, durable persistence, sequential execution across at least two genuinely free managed AI providers, persisted outputs, independent verification and revision, interruption recovery, final report generation, and safe pause/resume when quotas are exhausted.

The strongest **[RECOMMENDATION]** for a later prototype investigation is Supabase Free for Auth/Postgres/pgmq plus a lightweight Cloudflare Worker/Workers AI component. This is **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE**, not an approved architecture and not evidence that the MVP can yet meet MDL-002. It still depends on proving a second qualifying managed provider, confirming Nigeria onboarding and commercial/data terms, handling Supabase inactivity pausing and absent automatic backups, and verifying the exact quotas in the intended accounts.

Cloudflare Workers and Workflows offer the strongest documented execution and recovery primitives, but this research did not verify that the required Workflows and AI features can be used within a true $0 account. Vercel Hobby is technically capable in several respects but is expressly restricted to personal, non-commercial use. Render Free is explicitly not for production and does not provide free durable background execution. Mistral API is pay-as-you-go for API access; the reviewed material does not establish a free API entitlement. Gemini, Groq, and Cloudflare Workers AI are useful provider candidates, but the research did not establish two qualifying free managed entitlements for the intended workload and Nigerian entity.

No account or API was tested. All provider classifications below are documentary findings or explicitly marked inferences. No technology decision or ADR is approved by this report.

## 2. Binding financial constraints

The founder's constraint is a hard **₦0 monthly budget** for infrastructure and managed AI/API spend. It excludes paid subscriptions, card-dependent charges, temporary promotional credits, prepaid balances, and silently introduced paid dependencies. BYOK is not free managed access: any provider charges belong to the user who supplies the key and it is excluded from the proposed MVP unless the founder changes the sequencing decision.

A service advertised as “free” is not sufficient if the required capability is paid, the free tier is personal-only, a billing account must be activated, the allowance is promotional, or quota exhaustion makes the complete system stop without a safe recovery path. Each claim must therefore be evaluated at the combined-stack level.

## 4. Provider comparison

### 4.1 Google Gemini API / AI Studio — CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE

Google's documentation lists Nigeria as an available country for Google AI Studio and the Gemini API. Gemini supports function calling, structured tool calls, built-in tools, background interactions, polling, and stream reconnection. The Interactions API stores execution steps and supports chaining, but the documented retention is time-limited: one day on the free tier and 55 days on the paid tier. Google documents per-project RPM, TPM, and RPD limits, with 429/resource-exhaustion responses when limits are exceeded.

This is a capable inference/tool endpoint, not a durable cross-provider workflow controller. Collective would have to execute custom functions, persist canonical state, enforce authorization and idempotency, and reconcile interrupted calls. Google says unpaid-service content may be used to provide, improve, and develop products and ML technologies, with human review possible; sensitive, confidential, or personal information should not be submitted to unpaid services. Paid terms and stronger retention controls are separate and were not admitted into the ₦0 MVP.

Nigeria geographic availability is documented, but account/payment acceptance for a particular Nigerian entity, exact model quotas, commercial deployment details, and live API behavior were not tested. Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE as a provider component; not verified as free managed access for this MVP.**

### 4.2 GroqCloud API — CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE

Groq documents hosted inference, local tool calling, remote MCP tools, selected built-in tools, model capability tables, organization-level RPM/RPD/TPM/TPD limits, rate-limit headers, `429` responses, and `retry-after`/reset information. Its Services Agreement supports integrating the services into customer applications, subject to the agreement, model terms, fees, and policies.

Groq does not document a database, durable job store, checkpoint/replay engine, or cross-provider scheduler in the reviewed material. Those functions would belong to Collective. Groq's spend-limit documentation describes paid organization limits and warns that spend tracking can lag by 10–15 minutes; this is a guardrail, not a durable pause/resume mechanism. The reviewed official pages did not confirm Nigeria-specific eligibility, payment support, or a qualifying no-cost managed entitlement.

Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE as an inference/provider component; unverified for the binding free-managed-access requirement.** The historical Compound capability must not be selected: Groq documents that Compound was decommissioned on 2026-09-21.

### 4.3 Cloudflare Workers AI — CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE

Workers AI documents more than 50 open-source models, REST and Workers invocation, function calling for supported models, and a Free/Paid Workers allocation of 10,000 Neurons per day, with paid usage above the allowance. Model-specific limits vary and some frontier models require paid billing. Standard stateless inference is documented as having zero retention after processing and no training on Customer Content, subject to stateful features, logs, caches, uploaded files, third-party model terms, and selected model licenses.

Workers AI alone is one provider and does not establish the second provider required by MDL-002. It can be paired with an independently authorized provider only if that provider is genuinely available at ₦0 without BYOK, a payment card, promotional credit, or billing activation. Cloudflare documents a Lagos network presence, but that does not prove Nigerian account eligibility, payment, model availability, or commercial rights for every catalog model.

Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE as a free-limited provider component; not enough to establish a two-provider MVP.**

### 4.4 Mistral API / La Plateforme — NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 for the reviewed API route

Mistral documents Chat Completions, structured outputs, function calling, Agents, Conversations, connectors, code interpreter, batch jobs, rate limits, spending caps, and commercial terms. These are useful capabilities for a provider adapter. However, the official limits material says API access requires billing activation and pay-as-you-go. Free mode provides usage for Vibe and Studio but not API access, and there is no Free-mode API key in the reviewed documentation.

Mistral's data policy is also feature-dependent. Ordinary API input/output has a documented abuse-monitoring retention period; Agents, Conversations, Libraries, Files, and batch workflows are outside zero-data-retention. Nigeria account eligibility, payment acceptance, and residency were not confirmed.

Classification: **NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 for the intended managed API route unless new official evidence changes the entitlement.**

## 5. Database and authentication comparison

### 5.1 Supabase Free — CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE

Supabase Free provides hosted Postgres, Auth, Storage, Realtime, Edge Functions, Row Level Security, and Supabase Queues/pgmq. The documented Free allowances include 500 MB database size, 1 GB file storage, 5 GB egress, 50,000 monthly active users, 500,000 Edge Function invocations, 2 million Realtime messages, and 200 concurrent peak connections. Auth supports password, magic link, OTP, social sign-in, and SSO with JWT/RLS integration.

Supabase Queues is a strong documented fit for durable job state: messages have visibility timeouts, redelivery after worker interruption, archival, and replay patterns. The Free Edge Function duration is bounded at 150 seconds, so a workflow must be split into short, checkpointed steps. Free projects can pause after low activity over seven days; Free has no automatic database backups or PITR. Supabase documents available project regions but does not list Nigeria. Terms and commercial use require legal review rather than an assumption from the $0 price.

Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE as a prototype auth/state substrate; not verified for unattended production continuity at ₦0.**

### 5.2 Neon Free — CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE

Neon Free documents 100 projects, 100 compute-hours per project per month, 1 GB Postgres storage per project, 20 GB account storage, 5 GB public transfer per project, scale-to-zero, branches, pooled connections, Managed Better Auth, a Data API with JWT/RLS, and API-managed operations. It documents compute suspension when quotas are exhausted, with persistent suspension until explicit action or the billing-period reset. This can support an application-controlled paused state.

Neon is a database substrate, not a provider orchestrator. Scale-to-zero and failover reset session state; workflow state must be in ordinary tables. Free history and restore windows are limited, no Nigeria region is listed, and the reviewed official sources did not confirm Nigeria signup/payment or a special commercial-use grant.

Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE as a durable prototype database/auth substrate; not verified as a complete stack.**

Workers Free documents 100,000 requests per day, 128 MB memory, 10 ms CPU per invocation, 50 subrequests per invocation, and interruption risks for disconnected work. Cloudflare Workflows documents durable multi-step execution, persisted state, retries, pauses, event waits, and resume after interruption. D1, Durable Objects, R2, KV, Queues, and Hyperdrive provide persistence and coordination options.

The critical uncertainty is cost and entitlement: this research did not verify that the required Workflows and supporting products can be used in a true $0 account for the intended workload. Cloudflare account spending limits may reject invocations rather than gracefully pause the application. Nigeria onboarding and payment eligibility were not confirmed.

Classification: **CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE for the execution design; not verified as a complete ₦0 entitlement.**

### 6.2 Render Free — NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 for durable production execution

Render explicitly says Free instances are not for production applications. Free web services spin down after 15 minutes without inbound traffic, have ephemeral filesystems, cannot use persistent disks, and may restart. Free Postgres is limited to 1 GB, has no backups, expires after 30 days, and is deleted after a grace period unless upgraded. Free Key Value is in-memory. Render Workflows are the documented fit for agent/background orchestration, but the reviewed documentation prices workflow execution and state retention; it does not establish a Free Workflow plan.

Classification: **NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 for the required durable execution role.** It may be useful for a non-production demo coordinator only.

## 8. Complete-stack evaluations

### Stack A — Cloudflare Workers Free + Workers AI + D1/Durable Objects/Queues

**Verdict: NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 as a complete compliant stack.** Authentication requires application code or an external identity service; no complete end-user identity layer was established in core Workers. D1, Durable Objects, and Queues can persist state and coordinate jobs. Workers AI can provide one model provider and function calling. Sequential execution, output persistence, verification/revision, and final report generation are implementable as short idempotent steps.

The stack breaks at the verified two-provider gate: no second qualifying, genuinely free managed provider was established. Durable Workflows would improve interruption recovery, but free availability for the required arrangement is unverified. Without it, ordinary Workers interruption and account-level quota rejection require more application machinery than this stack proves at ₦0. Quota-safe pausing is architecturally possible with durable cursors and sleep/event primitives, but the free entitlement and Nigeria onboarding remain unresolved.

### Stack B — Supabase Free Auth/Postgres/Queues + Cloudflare Workers Free coordinator/AI

**Verdict: CONDITIONALLY FEASIBLE prototype baseline; NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 as a verified complete MVP stack.** Supabase provides the strongest documented auth layer through Auth, JWTs, and RLS. Postgres and pgmq can store objectives, step state, provider responses, idempotency keys, leases, checkpoints, audit events, verification results, revisions, and final reports. Cloudflare can host short coordinator steps and Workers AI can supply one provider.

The stack still breaks at MDL-002 because the evidence did not establish two qualifying no-cost managed inference providers. It is operationally fragile at ₦0: Supabase inactivity pausing, absent Free backups, finite quotas, database/full-disk failure modes, and bounded Edge Functions require careful design and external recovery/export procedures. The final report and revision loop are implementable only if the run is split into short queue steps and provider limits are treated as explicit state transitions.

### Stack C — Render Free or Vercel Hobby coordinator plus external providers

**Verdict: NOT FEASIBLE AT ₦0 for the target.** Render Free is explicitly non-production with weak durability; its native Workflows are not established as free. Vercel Workflows have a stronger technical fit, but Hobby is expressly non-commercial and has workflow event/data limits. Neither candidate supplies a verified pair of free managed AI providers, and external database/auth terms remain separate.

## 10. Privacy and Nigeria eligibility

The providers do not share one privacy posture. Gemini unpaid services may use content to improve products and may involve human review. Workers AI documents zero retention for standard stateless inference but has exceptions for stateful features, logs, caches, files, and third-party models. Mistral documents retention and training controls that vary by endpoint and feature. Supabase and Neon generally describe customer-data processing under customer instructions or agreements, but their regions and cross-border processing must be evaluated for Nigerian data.

Nigeria-specific evidence is uneven. Google explicitly lists Nigeria for Gemini API/AI Studio. Cloudflare documents a Lagos network presence, but this is not proof of account, payment, model, or feature eligibility. The reviewed official sources did not confirm Nigeria signup/payment eligibility for Groq, Mistral, Supabase, Neon, Render, or Vercel. No Nigerian data-residency option was established for the database candidates. These are unresolved onboarding and legal questions, not assumptions of exclusion.

Confidential or regulated information must not be routed through a free provider merely because its endpoint responds. Before public use, Collective needs a provider disclosure, lawful-basis and cross-border-transfer review, retention/deletion behavior, user controls, and a policy for provider/model selection by data class.

## 11. Quota exhaustion and recovery strategy

The common safe pattern is: reserve a bounded budget before dispatch; write a durable step checkpoint and idempotency key; call one provider; record the response and usage; advance the state transactionally; and only then acknowledge/remove the queue message. On 429, quota exhaustion, billing block, database pause, or storage failure, mark the run `paused`, persist the reason and earliest retry time, stop admitting new steps, and surface the limitation to the user.

Workers/Workflows can use retry, sleep, sleep-until, and external events where the entitlement exists. Supabase pgmq visibility timeouts can redeliver interrupted work. Neon documents persistent quota suspension that requires explicit action or reset. Render and Vercel document hard suspension or waiting behavior at some limits. None of these mechanisms removes the need for idempotency, provider-side reconciliation, export, alerting, and a manual recovery path.

Do not use synthetic keep-alive traffic as an assumed solution to inactivity suspension. Do not rely on an in-memory worker, temporary database table, provider conversation history, or client session as the system of record. Do not promise exactly-once external execution; use at-least-once delivery with idempotent commits as the P0.1 requirements state.

## 12. Hidden costs and upgrade traps

The main traps identified are: billing activation or pay-as-you-go requirements for model APIs; BYOK being mistaken for free managed access; promotional or prepaid credits being mistaken for recurring free service; personal-only hosting terms; free hosting that sleeps or loses local files; databases that expire, pause, or lack backups; workflow products whose durable state or execution is billed separately; quota exhaustion that suspends rather than queues; data-retention exceptions in stateful AI features; model-specific licenses; region/payment eligibility not implied by a public pricing page; and account-level spending limits that reject new work without preserving application state.

## 13. Blockers and unresolved questions

1. Which two model providers, if any, offer a genuinely free, recurring managed entitlement for the intended workload without BYOK, billing activation, promotional credits, or user-funded quota, and are they available to the intended Nigerian entity?
2. Can Cloudflare Workflows and required storage/AI features operate in a true zero-dollar account for the required workload?
3. Can Supabase Free remain active for unattended work without relying on undocumented keep-alive behavior, and what zero-cost backup/export path is acceptable?
4. Will each selected provider accept Nigerian signup, payment/tax details, and the intended commercial/data-processing use?
5. What exact model, request, token, storage, queue, workflow, and retention quotas apply to the intended accounts?
6. What provider-side cancellation, idempotency, duplicate suppression, and reconciliation mechanisms exist for every call?
7. What Nigerian privacy, cross-border transfer, sector, sanctions, and data-residency obligations apply?
8. Can a zero-cost deployment provide independent monitoring, alerting, export, and recovery when the primary database is paused, quota-exhausted, read-only, or deleted?
