# Phase 1.3 audience eligibility and catalogue scope audit

## Evidence freshness

The five supplied prior reports were read before this audit. The Phase 1 and Phase 1.2 materials carried forward evidence that ASUU activity is genuine but that the available 2026 call was a Federal University of Kashere (FUK) branch call, not a verified national application route. That carried-forward conclusion was independently tested against the current official ASUU website and official university sources on 7 November 2026. The FUK call was freshly reopened; the prior reports’ database values and earlier branch-specific interpretation are carried-forward production evidence, not a new database check in this note.

## Authenticity and provider identity

ASUU is the Academic Staff Union of Universities, a Nigerian university academic-staff union. Its official website identifies itself as the “official Website of Academic Staff Union of Universities” and warns about misinformation from phony websites. The same official homepage has an “INDIGENT SCHOLARSHIP” section stating that 158 indigent students benefited from an ASUU scholarship, and it links a scholarship-award post dated 27 August 2025. [1] The provider identity is therefore authentic at national-organization level, although the public evidence describes awards and branch activity rather than a single standardized national student application system.

Official university reporting independently corroborates ASUU branch scholarship activity. Ahmadu Bello University reported that its ASUU Zaria Chapter awarded scholarships to 22 ABU students, with the report dated 5 March 2025. [3] This supports a real ASUU-linked scholarship practice, but it is also branch/institution-specific evidence and does not establish universal eligibility for students at every Nigerian university.

## Audience and confirmed eligibility

The freshly reopened FUK call, published by the official Federal University of Kashere account on 20 September 2026, invited applications from “genuinely indigent FUK students” for the 2026 ASUU Indigent Scholarship Award. It required applicants to be registered FUK students for the current academic session, to demonstrate indigence, and to have at least a 3.0 CGPA from the preceding academic session. [2] These facts confirm a Nigerian undergraduate audience fit **where the student is enrolled at the participating FUK branch and meets the stated academic and financial criteria**.

The same call required an application letter, current student identity card, HOD letter confirming CGPA, last-semester course-registration form, and passport photograph. It directed applicants to submit a sealed envelope to the ASUU FUK Branch Secretariat or send a scanned PDF to the email addresses stated on the notice. [2] No fee is stated in the reopened call, and no paid-programme or intermediary-payment risk was identified in the official material reviewed.

The evidence does **not** establish a general national eligibility rule. It does not establish that every Nigerian citizen, every Nigerian university student, every university with an ASUU branch, or students studying outside Nigeria may apply through one central ASUU route. The FUK notice is expressly limited to registered FUK students. The ABU report similarly concerns ABU students. [2] [3] Do not generalize the FUK CGPA, document list, deadline, or submission route to all ASUU branches.

No official source reviewed states a nationality test beyond the Nigerian institutional context, a residence rule, a university-location rule applicable nationally, a universal academic-level rule, discipline restrictions, age limit, year-of-study restriction, or award amount for a standardized national scheme. The FUK notice establishes current-session university enrollment and a minimum CGPA, but it does not specify a discipline restriction or a general undergraduate year-of-study range. Omitted fields must remain unresolved, not treated as permitted or prohibited.

## Route provenance and application usefulness

No central national ASUU student application portal was verified. The official ASUU scholarship page currently extracts only the heading “Scholarship,” without a readable application form or route. [4] The official ASUU homepage provides scholarship-award information but no current universal student application workflow. [1]

The exact application route that could be reopened is the FUK branch route in the official FUK notice: physical submission to the ASUU FUK Branch Secretariat or scanned-PDF submission to the notice’s stated email addresses. [2] That route is authentic and institutionally attributable, but it is not a national route and it was not submitted or tested beyond reading the public notice.

For a Nigerian undergraduate, the record is useful only as a **branch-qualified lead**: the student must first verify that their university’s ASUU branch has issued a matching current call, then follow that branch’s official instructions. The record should not present the FUK route as a route for students at other universities. The carried-forward production record had no application URL and contained a deadline of 30 September 2026; the supplied reports identify that stored national-looking date as unsupported or requiring historical qualification. No new official source reviewed here validates a national 30 September deadline.

## Current-cycle status as of 7 November 2026

The freshly reopened FUK 2026 call set a deadline of Wednesday, 24 September 2026 at 12:00 noon and stated that late applications would not be considered. [2] That FUK application window is therefore closed by its published deadline as of the check date. The official ASUU homepage still displays scholarship-award information and the 2025 scholarship-award post, but it does not provide evidence of a currently open national student application window. [1] The official ASUU scholarship page likewise does not expose a current application route in the readable page content. [4]

This audit does not infer closure of every ASUU branch opportunity. It establishes only that no currently open, centrally administered ASUU undergraduate application route was verified, while the specifically reopened FUK 2026 route is historical/closed.

## Fees, risks, and unresolved questions

No application fee or paid programme was stated in the official FUK call or the official ASUU pages reviewed. [1] [2] Students should nevertheless be warned not to pay an intermediary based on this record, because the verified route is branch-mediated and no central portal was established.

Unresolved questions are: whether ASUU National has a current national student application process separate from branch calls; which universities/ASUU branches participate in any given cycle; whether eligibility is limited to students enrolled at a participating Nigerian university; whether future calls will retain the 3.0 CGPA threshold and document list; the applicable academic year/level and any discipline or year-of-study restrictions; the award amount and duration; and whether a new 2026/2027 or 2027 call exists after the FUK deadline. These cannot be answered from omitted fields or from the existence of the national website’s award references.

## Catalogue decision

**B — INCLUDE WITH RESTRICTIONS.** Keep the record only with an explicit warning that ASUU indigent scholarships are evidenced as branch- or institution-specific opportunities, not as a universal national application. Label the FUK 2026 route and 24 September 2026 deadline as historical/closed, do not publish the stored national-looking date as current, do not invent a central application URL, and direct students to verify a current call from their own university’s official ASUU branch. A future unqualified national listing should be held until ASUU publishes a centralized route or the catalogue adopts a clearly branch-specific structure.
