# Phase 1.3 Audience Eligibility and Catalogue Scope Audit

**Record:** Bode Amao Foundation Scholarship (BAF)  
**UUID:** `215ee2c9-28de-4609-b0b7-6898ae513a21`  
**Check date:** 2026-11-07  
**Classification:** **B — INCLUDE WITH RESTRICTIONS**  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium-high for provider identity and Nigerian undergraduate audience fit; medium for current-cycle status and application-route provenance.

## Determination

BAF is an authentic foundation programme with a current provider-controlled website, an official application-form page, and a provider-hosted PDF form. The provider states that its mission is to award scholarships to bright, indigent undergraduate Nigerian students in Nigerian universities. The official homepage further states that candidates must be registered Nigerian students, must be at least in 200 level when completing the form, and must have their university Registrar certify both indigence and registration for the current academic year. These facts establish confirmed fit for Nigerian undergraduates already studying in Nigeria, subject to the provider’s academic and documentation requirements. [1] [2] [3]

The record should remain in the initial Nigerian undergraduate catalogue only with restrictions. The exact submission route is unresolved: the official homepage says completed, Registrar-attested forms should be sent by post to BAF Secretariat, P.O. Box 7088, Secretariat Post Office, Ibadan, Oyo State, while the official form page says forms should be submitted by email to `chimakalu2001@yahoo.com` or `justinaoladoye@gmail.com`. Both pages are on the provider’s domain and were live at the check, but neither page explains which route supersedes the other. Students should not be given one route as definitively authoritative without editorial qualification. [1] [2]

## Authenticity and provider identity

The provider identifies itself as Bode Amao Foundation (BAF), a non-profit founded in 2002 by Chief Bode Amao, OON. Its official “About us” page describes support for indigent students across Nigerian geopolitical zones and Nigerian universities. The scholarship homepage and form page are on `bodeamaofoundation.org`, and the application PDF is hosted on that same domain. This is sufficient to treat the provider and programme as authentic for catalogue purposes, while not independently validating the foundation’s claimed impact figures. [2] [3]

## Confirmed audience and eligibility

The official homepage confirms the following:

- **Nationality:** applications are accepted only from registered Nigerian students. [1]
- **University location:** the stated audience is undergraduates in Nigerian universities; the provider does not present this as an overseas-university award. [1] [2]
- **Academic level:** undergraduate applicants must be at least 200 level when completing the forms. The opportunity therefore does not establish eligibility for prospective students, secondary-school students, or first-year students below 200 level. [1]
- **Academic condition:** high academic excellence is assessed using CGPA as the determinant factor. No numeric CGPA threshold is published on the current official homepage. [1]
- **Financial condition:** the candidate must be truly indigent, with certification by the Registrar of the relevant university. [1]
- **Current-enrolment condition:** the printed form must carry Registrar attestation that the applicant is registered for the current academic year. [1]
- **Discipline:** no subject, course, faculty, or STEM restriction is stated on the current official homepage. The provider-hosted form asks for course and institution details but does not publish a discipline limitation. Do not infer that every course is guaranteed funded; record the catalogue scope as “discipline not restricted in published criteria.” [1] [3]
- **Other stated constraints:** applicants are told to make one entry only, complete the form correctly, and understand that false information causes automatic disqualification. The award is described as per beneficiary per academic session and continuing through the course while high academic excellence is maintained. [1]

The provider-hosted PDF form requests course, course level, institution, CGPA by level, admission/course information, examination results, other scholarships or bonds, two referees, and institutional confirmation by the Head of Department, Dean, and Registrar. It also requests supporting materials including passport photographs and a letter of offer of admission. The PDF URL contains `/2024/10/`, which is a visible URL date, not proof that the form was published or updated in October 2024 and not proof that all requirements remain current for the present cycle. [3]

## Route provenance and application usefulness

The exact provider application route is available without submitting: the official form page links to the provider-hosted PDF. The form page states email submission to `chimakalu2001@yahoo.com` or `justinaoladoye@gmail.com`. The official homepage instead states that the printed form, supported by Registrar attestation, should be sent by post to BAF Secretariat at P.O. Box 7088, Secretariat Post Office, Ibadan, Oyo State. This contradiction is material because it affects where students send personal, academic, financial, and institutional information. The catalogue may link to the official form page and homepage, but must display both routes as conflicting provider instructions and must not silently choose one. [1] [2] [3]

The official homepage says forms are free of charge. No application fee or paid programme requirement is stated in the official sources reviewed. Students should nevertheless be warned not to pay an intermediary for the form or for submission, because “free of charge” applies to obtaining the application form and the provider does not publish a paid-service requirement. [1] [2]

## Current-cycle status

The official homepage and form page were accessible on 2026-11-07 and present the programme and a downloadable form, but they do not display a visible application opening date, closing date, academic-session label, or current-cycle announcement. Therefore, route reachability is confirmed, but current acceptance and cycle openness are not confirmed. HTTP accessibility is not treated as proof that applications are being processed.

A 5 November 2026 Facebook post by the third-party “Scholar Fahad” page describes a “Bode Amao Foundation Scholarship 2026,” states Nigerian undergraduate and 200-level eligibility, and gives a 31 December 2026 deadline. That post is not the provider’s official BAF website or an identified BAF-controlled social account, so it is useful as a lead only. It does not cure the absence of a current deadline on the official pages. The database’s stored `2026-12-31` deadline must therefore remain unconfirmed and must not be presented as an official provider-published deadline. [4]

**Current-cycle finding:** **Unresolved / not confirmed open.** The opportunity is useful as a verified provider and audience-fit lead with an accessible form, but the catalogue must label the cycle and deadline as unconfirmed until a provider-controlled current announcement or page establishes them.

## Restrictions and unresolved questions

The record should not be presented as a guaranteed opportunity for every Nigerian undergraduate. It is limited by the published 200-level minimum, Nigerian nationality and registration, Nigerian-university attendance, indigence certification, academic-performance condition, current-year registration attestation, one-entry rule, and any additional form/document requirements that remain current. The provider does not publish a numeric CGPA threshold or a discipline list on the current homepage. [1] [3]

The following remain unresolved: which submission route is authoritative (email or post); whether the 31 December 2026 date is an official current-cycle deadline; whether the provider is still accepting the provider-hosted form on 2026-11-07; whether the PDF form’s visible requirements remain unchanged; and whether the award amount, if any, is current. The reviewed official pages do not publish an award amount. Do not carry forward an amount from third-party summaries as provider-confirmed.
