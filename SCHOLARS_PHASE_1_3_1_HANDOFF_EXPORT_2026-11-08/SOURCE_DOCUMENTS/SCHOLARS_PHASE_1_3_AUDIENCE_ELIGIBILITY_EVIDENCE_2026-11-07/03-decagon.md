- **Check date:** 2026-11-07
- **Classification:** **B — INCLUDE WITH RESTRICTIONS**
- **Confidence:** High for the Foundation scholarship’s Nigerian undergraduate audience and current route; medium for the combined record because official Foundation and Institute pages describe materially different funding routes.

## Evidence freshness and controlling prior evidence

All five supplied prior reports were read before this audit. The Phase 1.2.1 JG Afrika addendum is not applicable to Decagon. The prior Decagon evidence was **freshly rechecked in Phase 1.1** and carried forward in Phase 1.2: the old stored `decagonfoundation.org/apply` route had been reported as stale/404, while the current official sites exposed both a Foundation scholarship route and an Institute Honours Programme route. This note adds a fresh 2026-11-07 check of both official domains and both linked application forms. No database, application, code, schema, configuration, deployment, or repository change was made.

## Authenticity and provider identity

The opportunity is authentic as an official Decagon offering: the Foundation’s official site identifies the programme as the Decagon Undergraduate Honors Program and states that it is run by Decagon Services Limited, with Decagon Foundation providing scholarships in collaboration with it. The Foundation’s official application page links to a Zoho-hosted form under a Decagon Services Limited account, while the Institute’s official application page links to a ZForms application titled “Decagon Honours Program - Direct Pay Application.” These are provider-linked routes, not third-party summaries. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

The record is nevertheless scope-sensitive. The Foundation route is a scholarship application. The Institute route is a direct-pay Honours Programme application, and its official fees page states that the applicant pays ₦275,000 per year to Decagon in addition to university tuition and school-related fees. The record must not merge these routes or present the paid route as the scholarship route. [2] [4] [5]

## Confirmed Nigerian undergraduate eligibility

The Foundation application page states that applicants must have sat JAMB/UTME, be Nigerian citizens currently residing in Nigeria, demonstrate financial need, and be seeking admission to or already holding an offer for an undergraduate degree programme at a Nigerian university. The Foundation student page separately states a minimum JAMB score of 250 or above, Nigerian citizenship, and application to a partner university. The Institute home page also states a 250+ UTME threshold, Nigerian citizenship, and that the applicant must not yet have started university. [1] [2] [3]

The exact Foundation form confirms a Nigerian-specific intake structure: it requires state of origin, LGA, Nigerian O-Level examination details, JAMB registration and score, NIN, state-of-origin certificate, and other Nigerian identity/education documents. The exact Institute form also requests Nigerian state of origin/LGA and lists Nigerian partner universities. These fields support route provenance and Nigerian targeting, but the audience finding rests on the provider’s express citizenship/residence and academic statements, not on omitted fields or form design alone. [4] [5]

Academic level is undergraduate/bachelor’s entry, not postgraduate. The Institute describes a four-year programme running alongside a degree, and the Foundation describes four years of tuition funding through graduation. The Institute route requires applicants who have not yet started university; the Foundation application wording allows applicants seeking admission or holding an undergraduate offer, so the safe catalogue wording should retain the pre-university/entry-stage constraint and not imply eligibility for students already enrolled unless the provider confirms it. [1] [2] [3]

## Discipline and other stated constraints

No single discipline restriction is published on the official Foundation pages. The exact Foundation form displays course choices including Accounting, Business Administration, Community Health Science, Computer Science, Criminology & Security Studies, Cyber Security, Economics, Human Anatomy, Human Physiology, Intelligence & Security Studies, International Relations, Mass Communication, Public Administration, Public Health, Software Engineering, and Information/Health Management, with Wellspring University shown as the partner-university choice on that form. The exact Institute form displays Wellspring and Christopher University and course menus that include business, computing, health, social-science, and humanities options. These are displayed route choices, not a basis to claim that every course or university is guaranteed for every applicant; placement is subject to provider selection and availability. [4] [5] [6]

The Foundation route requests JAMB and WAEC/NECO evidence, proof of financial need, a personal statement, identity documents, O-Level verification details, and other supporting documents. Its application page says applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and that tuition is paid directly to the institution. The Foundation student page describes merit, financial need, character/commitment, partner-university placement, and continuing funding against verified attendance and satisfactory progress. These requirements should be presented as current provider-stated requirements, not as guaranteed selection criteria. [1] [2]

[1] [3]

## Fees and paid-programme risk

The Foundation application and student pages describe a scholarship with tuition paid directly to the university, no loan/no repayment language, and a fully funded programme framing. The Foundation about page additionally says the programme covers tuition, accommodation, and living costs, but the current application page specifically confirms tuition and does not repeat all accommodation/living-cost details. This internal presentation difference means the catalogue should avoid promising accommodation or living-cost coverage without an additional current source. [1] [2] [6]

The Institute fees page is explicit that applicants pay ₦275,000 per year to Decagon for the Honours Programme, plus university tuition and school-related fees, both billed annually. The Institute home and application pages present this as the same four-year Honours Programme, while the Foundation pages describe Foundation-funded scholarships in collaboration with Decagon Services Limited. The relationship between a Foundation scholarship recipient and any Institute programme fee is not expressly resolved on the pages checked. This is the key restriction: applicants must be directed to the Foundation scholarship route and warned not to assume that the paid Institute route is fee-free or scholarship-funded. [2] [3] [5]

## Student usefulness and unresolved questions

**Student usefulness:** useful for Nigerian citizens currently residing in Nigeria who have not yet started an undergraduate degree, scored at least 250 in JAMB/UTME, and need financial support. The listing is not safe as an unrestricted “fully funded” scholarship CTA because the official routes differ and the Institute route is paid. The record should show the Foundation route as the application route, retain the Nigerian citizenship/residence and undergraduate-entry constraints, remove the unsupported 2026-10-15 deadline, and clearly separate or suppress the paid Institute route.

Unresolved questions are: whether every Foundation scholarship recipient is exempt from the ₦275,000 Institute fee; whether Foundation funding covers accommodation and living costs in the current cycle or only tuition; the definitive current partner-university/course availability for the Foundation intake; whether an applicant already enrolled in university is categorically ineligible under the Foundation route; and whether the current rolling/open status has a hidden cycle cutoff. No question should be answered by inference from a missing field or from the presence of an application form.

## Decision

**B — INCLUDE WITH RESTRICTIONS.** Include only as a Nigerian undergraduate scholarship lead through the official Decagon Foundation route. Do not classify it as an unrestricted fully funded award, do not use the paid Institute form as the scholarship application route, do not retain the unsupported 2026-10-15 deadline, and display a prominent fee-risk qualification until the Foundation/Institute funding relationship is explicitly resolved.
