# Scholars — Phase 1.3 Audience, Eligibility and Catalogue-Scope Audit

**Audit date / checked date:** 2026-11-07  
**Application:** `ImperioKDB/Scholars`  
**Scope:** 17-record Phase 1 target set; audience eligibility, application-route provenance, current-cycle status, and catalogue treatment.  
**Evidence basis:** the supplied structured audit results and the five supplied Phase 1/1.1/1.2 reports.  
**Report status:** final editorial audit; no production or application changes.

## Executive decision

The 17 records are not equivalent catalogue items. The safe Phase 1.3 decision is to classify them by audience fit and evidence state rather than expose all verified provider records as currently actionable Nigerian undergraduate opportunities.

Three records may remain in a Nigerian undergraduate catalogue only with explicit restrictions (B): ASUU, Bode Amao Foundation, and the Decagon Foundation scholarship route. Two records are outside the initial Nigerian undergraduate scope (C): JG Afrika, because Nigerian citizenship and university-location eligibility remain unresolved, and NDDC, because the verified 2026/2027 programme is a local postgraduate master’s scholarship, not an undergraduate opportunity. Five records require manual review or are insufficiently evidenced for a current catalogue decision (D): Lagos State Scholarship Board, Mastercard Foundation at PAU, Midwestern Oil & Gas, MTN Foundation, and NNPC–Renaissance. Seven are historical/closed and should be preserved only as dated leads (E): KPMG, Ejiro, Florence Bamidele Makanjuola, Light and Bells, NNPC/Seplat JV, Project 100 for 100, and PTDF. No record is classified A because no supplied record establishes an unrestricted, current, Nigerian-undergraduate route with fully resolved eligibility and status.

**Classification counts:** A = 0; B = 3; C = 2; D = 5; E = 7.

The evidence freshness labels are explicit. ASUU, Bode Amao, Ejiro, Florence, and JG Afrika were freshly rechecked in the Phase 1.3 workflow on 2026-11-07; Decagon, Light and Bells, and NDDC were freshly rechecked in Phase 1.1 and reconciled again; KPMG, Lagos State, Mastercard Foundation at PAU, Midwestern, MTN, NNPC–Renaissance, NNPC/Seplat, Project 100, and PTDF received supplemental official-source rechecks on 2026-11-08 after the initial workflow exhausted its credit budget. The report preserves these dates and does not describe carried-forward evidence as a fresh source check.

No production/application changes were made. No database, code, schema, configuration, deployment, or catalogue write occurred. No application was submitted, and no CAPTCHA or protected application workflow was bypassed.

## Classification policy

- **A — INCLUDE, CURRENT AND UNRESTRICTED:** current route and Nigerian undergraduate eligibility are sufficiently established for ordinary catalogue treatment. None in this set.
- **B — INCLUDE WITH RESTRICTIONS:** useful to Nigerian students, but branch, route, funding, or current-cycle limitations must be displayed prominently.
- **C — EXCLUDE FROM INITIAL NIGERIAN UNDERGRADUATE CATALOGUE:** authentic opportunity, but outside the catalogue audience or academic scope, or nationality/location eligibility is unresolved in a way that makes ordinary inclusion unsafe.
- **D — MANUAL REVIEW / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** provider or programme may be genuine, but current eligibility, route, or lifecycle cannot be safely represented.
- **E — HISTORICAL / CLOSED:** the latest evidenced cycle is closed or historical; preserve only as a dated lead and do not imply a future or current opening.

## Cross-record catalogue and data-policy recommendations

1. **Use a lifecycle-aware catalogue, not a single verified flag.** A record should separately represent provider authenticity, audience fit, route provenance, current-cycle state, and evidence freshness. `verified = true` must not by itself imply current Nigerian undergraduate actionability.
2. **Require a structured evidence package per record.** At minimum: exact provider source URL(s), source publication date or “unavailable,” checked date, freshness label (`freshly rechecked` or `carried forward`), confirmed eligibility, unresolved eligibility, route status, current-cycle status, classification, confidence, and limitations.
3. **Keep historical cycles visibly historical.** Store cycle-specific requirements and dates with the cycle they describe. Never turn a prior deadline into a future date by shifting the year. Do not infer a precise closure timestamp from a past deadline.
4. **Separate audience and academic level.** Undergraduate catalogue inclusion must require undergraduate fit. NDDC’s local postgraduate master’s scholarship belongs outside an undergraduate catalogue. JG Afrika must not be treated as Nigerian-audience eligible without provider confirmation.
5. **Separate scholarships from paid programmes.** Decagon Foundation scholarship and Decagon Institute paid Honours Programme must have distinct routes and student-facing labels. The Institute’s ₦275,000 annual fee plus tuition/school fees must never be hidden inside a scholarship listing.
6. **Do not generalize local evidence.** ASUU FUK branch rules are evidence for FUK’s branch call only, not a national ASUU route. Bode Amao’s conflicting postal/email routes must remain explicit until the provider resolves them.
7. **Treat route reachability and acceptance as different facts.** A reachable page or login screen is not proof that a form accepts applications. NDDC’s 403/access anomaly is source-unavailable evidence, not proof of closure. JG Afrika’s displayed form is apparently open, but end-to-end acceptance was not tested.
8. **Do not expose unsupported dates.** The stored future dates for Florence, KPMG, Lagos State, Midwestern, MTN, NNPC–Renaissance, NNPC/Seplat, and PTDF are not published future facts in the supplied evidence. The JG Afrika 2027-01-01 date is a notification/non-selection date, not a deadline.
9. **Add a manual-review state and a source-unavailable state.** The current schema lacks dedicated fields for uncertainty, route reachability, source review, paid-programme taxonomy, and historical cycle. Until a later approved schema phase, preserve these distinctions in editorial notes and do not flatten them into open/closed.
10. **Recommended scalable catalogue policy:** publish only A/B records in the Nigerian undergraduate catalogue; publish B records with mandatory restriction banners; route C records to a separate international, South Africa-specific, postgraduate, or out-of-scope catalogue; keep D records unpublished or manual-review-only; retain E records in a dated archive/monitoring feed. Require a same-day source recheck before any future status write.
