## Findings

The foundation’s own website describes its undergraduate scholarship, eligibility criteria, and a downloadable application form. This establishes that the program exists. The homepage says applicants must be registered Nigerian students, be at least in 200 level, demonstrate academic excellence based on CGPA, and obtain registrar certification of indigence and current-year registration. It says forms are free, one entry only, and presents postal submission to the foundation’s secretariat. The official application-form page, however, says forms should be emailed to chimakalu2001@yahoo.com or justinaoladoye@gmail.com. Because the official pages conflict about the submission channel, the complete current submission instructions cannot safely be drafted.

The official PDF form includes fields for academic records and two referees, calls for three passport photographs, asks for a letter of admission, and includes institutional confirmation/signature fields. The previously stored claimed attachments (transcript, references, ID, LGA letter, medical certificate/HIV test, JAMB/admission/course-registration documents) are not all established by the current official page text. Some broad items overlap with form fields, but these do not validate the complete stored checklist. No official page reviewed states a 2026/27 opening, a deadline of 2026-12-31, or that applications are open now. Do not infer open status from a reachable page or the form’s availability.

The stored URL is the foundation’s official homepage, is reachable in page fetching, and links to its application page. It is provider-linked and no authoritative evidence supports changing it. This assesses destination accuracy separately from whether a current cycle is open.
