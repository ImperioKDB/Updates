# Project 100 for 100 STEM Scholarship — revalidation evidence

**Audit date:** 2026-11-07
**Scope:** Read-only public-source review; no production data changed.

## Findings

Afro-Tech Girls’ official announcement establishes that Project 100 for 100 is a real program: its 2026 call offered a one-time ₦100,000 scholarship to 100 female undergraduate students studying STEM-related degrees in Nigeria. The official announcement gives eligibility and a 25 September 2026 deadline. The provider-linked campaign page supplies further requirements and a dated process schedule. Its headline text still says “The application is open,” but the page’s own dates have passed. The Google Form linked by that page resolves to the same stored form and says it is no longer accepting responses. As of the audit date, the 2026 application is closed; the sources reviewed do not establish a later cycle. The form destination is genuine and provider-linked, but not currently accepting applications.

## Supported and unresolved claims

**Supported:** The program exists; the 2026 cycle’s award, eligibility, deadline, application materials, and published schedule above; the form’s provider connection and closed response status; the stored URL is provider-linked and resolves to the form referenced by the official campaign page.

**Unresolved:** No next-cycle opening or deadline is announced in the inspected official materials. No exact timestamp/date for when the Google Form stopped accepting submissions is shown. The published schedule runs through 6 November 2026, but no distinct cycle-close date is stated. The page’s “application is open” banner is stale or inconsistent with both its elapsed timeline and the closed form.
