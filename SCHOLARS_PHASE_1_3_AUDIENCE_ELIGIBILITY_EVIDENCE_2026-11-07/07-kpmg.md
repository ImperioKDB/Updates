## Confirmed Nigerian undergraduate eligibility (2026 cycle)

The official KPMG Nigeria announcement states that applications were open to **100-level students** studying **Accounting, Business Administration, Economics, Actuarial Science, Insurance, or Finance** at **Federal Universities in Nigeria** [1]. The linked 2026 listing adds these requirements: a provisional admission letter from a Federal University in Nigeria into a four-year course in one of those disciplines; attendance at a public secondary school, or a private secondary school where the student received a scholarship; a minimum UTME score of 230; at least five WASSCE distinctions including English and Mathematics; and 100-level status at application [2].

Accordingly, the 2026 evidence confirms Nigerian university location and Federal University restriction, undergraduate entry level, the six named disciplines, four-year-course wording, academic thresholds, and the stated secondary-school constraint [1] [2]. The programme’s job summary describes the audience as indigent Nigerian students currently admitted into a Federal University in Nigeria [2].

Nationality is stated as “Nigerian” in the programme description, so Nigerian audience fit is confirmed for the 2026 cycle [2]. The sources do not provide a separate passport/citizenship-document rule, and they do not establish eligibility for non-Nigerian nationals. Residence outside Nigeria, transfer students, students in state/private universities, postgraduate students, non-100-level students, and applicants in disciplines outside the six listed fields are not confirmed eligible by these sources [1] [2].

## Fees, paid-programme risk, and student usefulness

No application fee or paid-programme charge is stated in the official 2026 announcement or the linked KPMG application listing [1] [2]. The sources describe financial support, mentorship, and internship opportunities, but do not state an award amount, duration, payment mechanics, or whether tuition, living costs, or other expenses are fully covered [1] [2]. Students should therefore not be promised a particular monetary amount or coverage scope.

For students seeking an open opportunity, the record is **not currently useful for applying**: the exact 2026 route is expired, the stored URL is an older 2025 listing, and no 2027 route is verified [2] [3]. It remains useful as a historical eligibility reference and as a lead to watch for a future official KPMG Nigeria announcement, but a future-cycle listing should not be created from the stored dates [2] [4].
