## Decision and unresolved questions

**Decision: C — EXCLUDE FROM INITIAL NIGERIAN UNDERGRADUATE CATALOGUE.** This classification does not find the bursary illegitimate. It reflects a real, provider-hosted 2027 bursary whose Nigerian audience fit has not been established. Listing it in a Nigerian undergraduate catalogue would risk implying that Nigerian citizens studying in Nigeria are eligible when the provider has not confirmed nationality or university-location rules. The record may be reconsidered if JG Afrika confirms Nigerian eligibility and the applicable university-location rule, or if the catalogue owner adopts a clearly labelled South Africa/international scope with an explicit warning. [Official bursary page](https://www.jgafrika.com/careers-offered-at-jg-afrika/bursary/) [Official LinkedIn announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jg-afrika-pty-ltd_jgafrika-bursaryopportunities-activity-7496086303910985729-x31d)

Unresolved questions are: whether South African citizenship is mandatory; whether Nigerian citizens and other African citizens may apply; whether applicants must attend a South African university or may attend an accredited university elsewhere; whether the full current-university transcript rule applies to every applicant including entrants from Matric; whether the form is currently accepting submissions end-to-end; and whether an unpublished closing date exists. The official materials also do not state an award amount or living-cost coverage. [Official bursary page](https://www.jgafrika.com/careers-offered-at-jg-afrika/bursary/)
