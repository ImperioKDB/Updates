# PTDF In-Country Undergraduate Scholarship Scheme

The official PTDF guidelines confirm a 2026/27 in-country scheme for qualified Nigerians in Nigerian public universities. Undergraduate applicants had to be full-time 200-level students in sponsored oil-and-gas, energy, or management-related courses, with WAEC/SSCE/NECO credits, minimum CGPA 2.4/5.0, first-year transcript, admission letter, indigeneship certificate, and a 300-word statement. The official application window was 27 April–5 June 2026. No 2027 cycle or stored 2027 dates were supported.
