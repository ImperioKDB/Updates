## Evidence status and provenance

The Phase 1.2 reports carried Florence forward from the earlier evidence package; it was not independently reopened during Phase 1.2. The earlier evidence reported that Opekete Foundation’s official page described the scholarship, linked a Google Form, and showed a 1 May–22 May 2026 window, while the linked form said it was no longer accepting responses. The supplied record’s `opens_at` (2027-05-01) and `deadline` (2027-05-22) were not supported by the provider sources and must not be treated as published dates. This note freshly reopens the official provider pages and the exact linked form on 2026-11-07.

Opekete Foundation identifies the programme as the Florence Bamidele Makanjuola Scholarship / FBM Scholarship for Women. Its official application page names the Foundation, explains that it was founded by Florence Bamidele Makanjuola, gives a Lagos, Nigeria address and official contact details, and links directly to the provider-authorized Google Form. The official homepage likewise identifies the fourth FBM scholarship and links its Apply Now buttons to the same form. [1] [2] [3]

The exact route provenance is therefore established as: Scholars destination `https://opeketefoundation.org/apply` → provider page → `https://forms.gle/P13HSQaVUZoaoG8V7` → Google Form titled **“2026/27 Opekete Foundation Scholarship Application Form.”** The form states that it was created inside Opekete Foundation. No application was submitted. [1] [4]

## Confirmed Nigerian undergraduate audience fit

The provider page states that beneficiaries must be a **female Nigerian citizen**. It states that an applicant must have gained admission to, or be currently enrolled in, a public university or Senior Secondary School, and must be attending or intending to attend a public or federal-government-owned Senior Secondary School or University. [1]

For university applicants, the provider states a minimum of five distinctions (A1–B3) in English, Mathematics, and three other major subjects in WAEC/NECO at one sitting, plus meeting the required JAMB cut-off for the course and school applied to. These are official university-applicant constraints, not assumptions from omitted fields. [1]

The provider limits the stated university study purpose to **Teaching, Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (STEM), or Nursing**. The homepage describes the programme as supporting young women studying or planning to study STEM Education or Nursing. No broader discipline eligibility should be added. [1] [2]

The provider states maximum support of ₦650,000 per annum for university students and ₦350,000 per annum for secondary-school students. These are stated maximum scholarship amounts, not a guarantee of award or proof that a current cycle is funded. The reviewed pages do not state an application fee or a paid-programme requirement; no paid-programme risk was evidenced. [1]

The university-location rule is only partly explicit. The page requires attendance at, or intended attendance at, a public or federal-government-owned university, but it does not separately publish a more specific city/state rule or an explicit sentence saying that the university must be physically located in Nigeria. The Nigerian-citizenship requirement and the Foundation’s Nigeria-focused institutional description support Nigerian audience relevance, but the catalogue should not invent a more detailed location rule. [1] [2]

## Current-cycle status and application usefulness

The official application page currently displays the 2026 cycle as opening on 1 May 2026 and closing on 22 May 2026. The official homepage repeats the 2026 cycle messaging. Those dates have passed as of the 2026-11-07 check date. Neither reviewed provider page displays a later 2027 opening or deadline. Publication dates for these pages are unavailable. [1] [2]

The exact provider-linked Google Form is not currently usable for an application: it says, **“The form 2026/27 Opekete Foundation Scholarship Application Form is no longer accepting responses.”** This is direct route-status evidence, not an inference from HTTP status. The route was inspected without submission. [4]

Accordingly, the listing is useful only as a **historical eligibility reference and provider-monitoring lead** at this check date. It should not be presented as an open application opportunity. The official `/apply` page remains a legitimate provider information route, but its current banner is stale for the purpose of live applications. [1]

## Unresolved questions

The provider has not published a confirmed next-cycle announcement, opening date, closing date, or current application route beyond the closed 2026/27 form. It is unresolved whether a subsequent cycle will run, whether the eligibility criteria or award amounts will change, and whether the provider will refresh the `/apply` page. The current sources also do not re-establish a complete future-cycle document checklist or explain why the 2026/27 form stopped accepting responses. [1] [2] [4]

## Decision

**E — HISTORICAL / CLOSED.** The programme is authentic and its published criteria confirm a potentially relevant Nigerian female undergraduate audience, but the only exact application route is closed and no later cycle is officially evidenced. Do not publish the stored 2027 dates as official, do not label the route open, and do not imply that the closed form accepts submissions. A future reclassification would require a fresh official cycle announcement and a newly opened provider-controlled route.
