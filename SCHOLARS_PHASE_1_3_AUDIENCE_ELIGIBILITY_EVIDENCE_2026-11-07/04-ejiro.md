The opportunity is authentic as a provider programme. The first-party website identifies itself as the Ejiro M Ukiri International Scholarship Fund, says the fund is headquartered in the United States, names Ejiroghene Mary Ukiri as founder, and publishes a fund contact email. The provider’s About page describes a mission to support graduating Nigerian Senior Secondary School students pursuing university/college education in Nigeria, nationwide and regardless of state of origin or background. Publication dates for these pages are unavailable; accessed 2026-11-07. [1] [2]

The provider’s site navigation and the official 1 September 2026 and 30 September 2026 provider Instagram announcements identify `https://www.ejirointernationalscholarshipfund.com/apply` as the application route. The provider `/apply` page was reachable on 2026-11-07 but its extracted content contained only the site header/navigation and no visible form or current instructions. Its reachability is not evidence that applications are open. Publication date for the page is unavailable. [3] [4] [5]

The stored route, `https://form.jotform.com/230343573288055`, is a third-party Jotform. It is plausibly associated with the fund because its page names the scholarship, but the exact form ID was not found linked from the current official pages. ## Nigerian undergraduate audience fit and confirmed constraints

The programme has confirmed Nigerian audience relevance, but the latest official cycle was aimed primarily at entrants rather than currently enrolled undergraduates. The current provider homepage states that applicants must be Nigerian citizens, intend to pursue further education after graduation in Nigeria, be graduating Senior Secondary School (SS3) students, and have access to a current report card. It also says the fund supports students living in Nigeria who seek university, college, polytechnic, or other higher education. Publication date is unavailable. [1]

The provider’s official 1 September 2026 cycle announcement stated that applications were for Nigerian students currently attending or intending to pursue a post-secondary degree in Nigeria, and required an online application, a minimum 500-word essay, the most recent report card, and following the provider’s Instagram account. The announcement was published 2026-09-01. [4] The official 30 September 2026 announcement stated that 30 September was the last day to apply and again directed applicants to the provider `/apply` route. Published 2026-09-30. [5]

The provider therefore confirms Nigerian citizenship/nationality and Nigeria-based intended study for the cited cycles. It does not establish a foreign-university route. The homepage expressly says further education in Nigeria, and the 2026 announcement likewise described study in Nigeria. [1] [4] The evidence does not establish a state-of-residence restriction; the About page instead says the fund aims to support students nationwide regardless of state of origin and background. [2]

Academic level is clearly higher-education entry or transition from secondary school in the homepage criteria: graduating SS3 applicants intending further education. [1] The 2026 announcement used broader wording (“currently attending or intending to pursue a post-secondary degree”), so it may have included some current post-secondary students, but that broader wording does not prove that every currently enrolled undergraduate was eligible or that it supersedes the SS3 criterion for a future cycle. [4] Discipline/field restrictions were not stated in the reviewed official sources. The provider’s winners gallery shows awards across Medicine and Surgery, Nursing, Pharmacy, Engineering, English and Literature Studies, Economics, Agricultural Economics, Neuroscience, and related programmes, which supports broad disciplinary scope but is not a formal future-cycle eligibility rule. Publication date for the gallery is unavailable; accessed 2026-11-07. [7]

The published award description says the fund intends to cover first-year university costs up to ₦150,000. This is a stated award ceiling/intent, not proof of a guaranteed award for every applicant. No application fee or paid-programme requirement is stated on the reviewed official pages or 2026 announcement. [1] [4] The fund’s public donation page is not an applicant payment instruction; applicants should not be directed to pay an intermediary or fee based on this evidence. [1]

## Current-cycle status and student usefulness

The latest dated official cycle ran from 2026-09-01 through 2026-09-30. The 2026-09-30 provider announcement called that date the last application day. [4] [5] The stored Jotform currently says the scholarship is closed and is not taking applications. [6] No official 2027 opening date, deadline, or requirements were found on the current provider homepage, About page, gallery, or `/apply` route during the 2026-11-07 check. The blank `/apply` page is not evidence of closure by itself; the explicit closed message on the stored form and the expired official 2026 deadline are the basis for the closed classification. [3] [6]

The record is useful only as a clearly labelled historical lead for Nigerian students who are graduating SS3 or otherwise match the dated 2026 announcement. It is not currently actionable for a student seeking an open application. Any catalogue entry should state that the 2026 cycle closed on 2026-09-30, avoid presenting the 2026 essay/report-card requirements as current, and avoid publishing an unsupported 2027 date. The provider `/apply` URL is the better provenance link because the provider’s own announcements point there, but it should not be presented as an open form. [3] [4] [5]
